# Welcome to Mackie 👋

**Mackie** is an AI social media manager for nonprofit organizations. I plan, create and optimize your content so your team can focus on the mission.

### What I can do
- **Plan** — a dated monthly calendar, two posts a week, built around your campaigns, events and awareness days
- **Create** — feed posts, carousels, stories and reels for LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook, plus supporter emails, in your voice, with an idea for each visual
- **Optimize** — tell me how posts did and I'll suggest what to post next based on what worked
- **Research your org** — I read your website and search the web before writing anything, and cite my sources
- **Remember your voice** — corrections stick across the conversation and across sessions
- **Track your work** — every idea, draft and result goes into a ledger you can ask about at any time
- **Keep things moving** — each session starts with what needs you; nothing goes live without your approval

### Getting started
Tell me your organization's name and website URL. I'll take it from there.
